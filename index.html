Daily target

How many prospects to work each day. Start conservative (20) on a healthy account and raise it gradually as things stay stable — sudden jumps in cold DMs are what triggers Instagram limits.

Comment A/B test

Half your prospects are randomly flagged to comment on their post first; half are DM-only. This compares reply rates so you can see whether commenting is worth the effort. Give it 100+ in each group before trusting it.
